# Tempo and beat timing

Use the bottom-left corner for the shared BPM field, Tap Tempo, Sync and four beat lights. BPM accepts 30–300. Tap at least twice; the last eight taps are averaged. A gap over 2.5 seconds starts a fresh tap session. Very rapid taps are ignored for the tempo estimate.

In Live → Effects, choose Seconds or Beats for each effect. Beat cycles support ¼, ½, 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 beats. A chase completes its entire fixture sequence in the chosen beat count. In Seconds mode, chase rate is per step; other effects use full cycles.

Sync resets both timing modes together. BPM changes preserve the accumulated beat position. All beat effects—including layered effect presets—use the shared clock. Tempo and effect timing save in the show; older shows keep their seconds timing and default to 120 BPM.

Add Tap Tempo or Sync Tempo actions to custom board buttons. Cue fades and sequence holds remain measured in seconds. Microphone tempo following and experimental music Autopilot are available; see AUTOPILOT.md. MIDI clock and Ableton Link are not implemented.
