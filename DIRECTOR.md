# Music director prototype

Open `Examples/AYRA-NGEAR-rig.lumendesk.json` with Open show. It patches AYRA MH1 at 1, N-GEAR MH3 at 10, N-GEAR MH4 at 19, AYRA MH2 at 28, all in 9-channel mode. Profiles are embedded and appear in Patch & Output. Match the physical fixtures' modes and addresses before enabling output. The two profiles remain provisional and need hardware checks.

The Laserworld EL-400RGB MK2 is patched at address 64 (channels 64–72), using the supplied nine-channel manual. Select LA1 in Design → Color for manual mode, pattern, position, speed, size and colour-slot controls. It starts Off, is always excluded from Autopilot regardless of its role setting, and sends zero laser channels on Blackout, Master zero or Stop. The supplied manual has no named slot table. Named colours and scanning presets use the operator-confirmed values: Rainbow 0, White 2, Purple 4, Light blue 6, Green 8, Blue 10, Red 12; Double 127, Vertical 135, Vertical double 145, Diagonal 94, Spinning 100, Single 116. These are available in Design and on the editable live board. Sound/Automatic/DMX Dynamic and Laser OFF buttons change only the laser, preserving head playback. Raw DMX values remain editable. Hardware verification is pending. Laser patterns are not simulated in the stage preview.

Patch & Output has an Autopilot role per fixture: Beam accents, Wash atmosphere, or Manual only. Old shows infer beam for moving heads and wash for other fixtures. Assign any subsequently added laser Manual only.

Autopilot cycles through mirrored sweep, outward fan, crossed beams, and alternating accents. Major looks and colours change every eight bars (32 beats), with four-beat movement/colour transitions. Beam pairs mirror each other; washes use calmer travel and steadier intensity. Break/drop energy estimates modulate the look. The phrase clock follows BPM; use Sync on a musical downbeat. It does not infer the track's true phrase start or predict future drops. Auto style remains a tonal heuristic, not a trained genre model.

Choose Microphone or Computer audio in the Autopilot panel, then press Listen. Computer audio captures sound playing on the Mac using ScreenCaptureKit and macOS screen/system-audio permission; only audio samples are handled, with no recordings or screen images saved. Changing the source stops listening and Autopilot, then requires another explicit Listen. Use Try demo to rehearse without capture permission. Save this look creates an editable cue from the generated colours, intensity and movement, before Master/Blackout, so those controls are not baked into the cue. It saves a static look, not the motion animation. Turn Autopilot off before replaying it. Assign saved cues to buttons through Edit board. Master/Blackout/Stop retain priority.

Live microphone accuracy and hardware output need real-rig verification. Song-file analysis, natural-language scene generation, learned genre recognition, and predictive section planning are future work.
